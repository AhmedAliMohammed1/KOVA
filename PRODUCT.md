# KOVA Scrubs product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform and users

Bilingual responsive web commerce for Egyptian healthcare professionals and medical students, primarily using mobile browsers during shifts or breaks.

## Purpose and positioning

KOVA presents medical apparel with a premium, quiet editorial identity: navy, teal, real studio photography and clear Arabic/English shopping journeys. The storefront is Arabic-first; English stays explicitly available. Do not invent manufacturing, antimicrobial, waterproof, fabric-performance or availability claims from photographs or legacy brand copy.

## Approved catalog

The approved grouping is one women's scrub set in grey/blue/burgundy and a separate black men's set. Base price EGP 850 and COD deposit EGP 200 were owner-approved. Current products, sizes, prices, variants and stock are database-authoritative and editable in admin; see the dated [project state](PROJECT_STATE.md). Demo and legacy products are not current KOVA merchandise.

## Core journeys

- Discover/filter products, select a colour, inspect its gallery and size guide, choose an available variant.
- Persist bag/wishlist, merge authenticated selections and manage customer delivery addresses.
- Review server-calculated shipping, promotions, deposits and totals before checkout.
- Submit private manual-transfer proof; track orders, payment/fulfilment status, invoices, returns and eligible reviews.
- Staff operate orders, inventory, products, CMS, shipping, discounts, sizing, reviews and commercial workflows through role-scoped administration.
- Super admins alone manage staff roles; customer access is automatic.

## Constraints

Egypt-only delivery and Egyptian mobile validation. Money uses integer piastres. Stock reservations, prices, discounts and payment transitions are server/database-controlled. Vodafone Cash/InstaPay/COD proof workflows exist; Paymob remains held pending acceptance. Phone OTP is separately configurable and was explicitly disabled for the current store. Email verification is independent.

No material, care, legal policy, stock level or provider readiness should be inferred from legacy Scrub Vibe documentation. Business sign-off and remaining sandbox acceptance are tracked in [TASKS](TASKS.md).
